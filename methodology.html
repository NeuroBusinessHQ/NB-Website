NeuroBusiness methodology and research status

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

The NeuroBusiness Intelligence Framework (NBIF) is a proprietary business workstyle model. The repository documents NBIF 1.1 Cognitive Pilot as a 50-question instrument across five dimensions. Published independent psychometric validation is not established by the documentation reviewed for this page.

Four different kinds of statements

Research evidence
External studies support only the claims they actually investigate. They do not automatically validate NBIF.
NeuroBusiness model
A proprietary structure used to organize self-reported work patterns and practical reflection.
Hypothesis
An interpretation to check against experience and task evidence, rather than a proven fact about a person.
Product function
A behavior implemented in software or explicitly included in an agreed service. A concept name alone does not establish availability.

NBIF 1.1 Cognitive Pilot

The five documented dimensions concern energy and visibility; cognition and decision-making; motivation; implementation and scaling; and stress regulation and self-leadership. Responses support a workstyle profile. They do not measure brain structure, establish a clinical diagnosis or objectively certify competence.

NeuroBusiness Types

Strategist, Visionary, Builder, Connector and High Performer are interpretive profile labels. They are not empirically established discrete classes. The repository contains differing H/G labels for High Performer; interpretation should refer to the instrument and scoring version, not only the letter. The internal audit also identifies overlap between performance-related interpretation and strain indicators.

AI Readiness Framework

The proposed framework separates use-case clarity, people and skills, leadership and work design, data and tools, and governance. Use an evidence register with four descriptive states: not examined, gap identified, ready for a bounded pilot, and demonstrated in that pilot. These are working categories, not validated scales or population norms. NBIF scores must not be converted into an AI readiness score.

Team Map

A descriptive view of team work patterns and contributions. The existing Team Intelligence offer provides an aggregated team profile and contribution matrix. A separate released artifact named Team Map has not been verified.

Leadership Guide

A proposed structure for decision rights, briefing expectations, feedback and escalation. Existing communication and task-briefing guides are described in the team offer; a standalone product under this exact name is not verified.

Delegation Matrix

A proposed task-owner-reviewer table with permitted AI use and stop conditions. It is a working method, not an automated assignment engine. Do not infer delegation authority from a profile label.

Conflict Snapshot

A proposed record separating the observable disagreement, each person’s interpretation, the work context and an agreed next step. No separately released diagnostic measure with this name was found. It must not be used to diagnose individuals or predict conflict.

What remains to be established

The internal July 2026 audit recommends cognitive pretests, item analysis, reliability work, factor analysis and comparison with relevant constructs. It is a plan, not evidence that those studies have been completed. Publish sample definitions, methods, scoring versions, limitations and results before making validation claims.

Appropriate use

Use reports as a basis for reflection and voluntary development. Do not use them alone for hiring, promotion, exclusion, medical decisions or AI access eligibility. Reassess interpretations when role, workload or context changes.
