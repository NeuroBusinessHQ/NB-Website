About NeuroBusiness

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

NeuroBusiness develops business workstyle diagnostics and Team Intelligence to support reflection on work, collaboration and leadership. Its AI knowledge pages examine how those human questions intersect with organizational AI adoption.

Organization and founder

The existing NeuroBusiness website identifies the German Institute of Positive Psychology as its parent organization and Eva Kolontai as its founder. The legal provider information is linked from Legal / Imprint. Read the founder profile.

Current offers

Solo diagnostics, Team Intelligence and the Practitioner offer are the existing public offer routes. AI readiness assessment scope is discussed separately; the enterprise context layer is a product direction.
