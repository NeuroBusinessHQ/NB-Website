Leadership Intelligence and AI readiness

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

Leadership Intelligence at NeuroBusiness describes the application of workstyle reflection to leadership decisions, communication and delegation. It does not denote a separately validated leadership ability score.

Leadership questions worth assessing

Who decides when AI may be used? Who reviews its output? Which decisions require a person’s approval? How can employees raise concerns without being treated as resistant? Answer these with examples from recent work, rather than general confidence ratings.

From assessment to a leadership guide

A useful guide records decision responsibilities, briefing preferences, feedback routines and situations requiring escalation. The existing team offer includes communication and task-briefing support. A separately named Leadership Guide is not documented here as a released standalone product.

Delegation requires context

For each task, record the owner, reviewer, permitted AI assistance, required input and stop conditions. This is a proposed working template, not an automated delegation decision or a validated instrument. Review the agreement with the people doing the task.
