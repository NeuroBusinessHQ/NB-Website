Enterprise AI: human context and product direction

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

The NeuroBusiness enterprise direction is a human context layer: helping organizations connect AI use with work patterns, team context and leadership decisions. The broader integration layer is a product vision, not a currently available enterprise connector platform.

Available now

The existing website offers business diagnostics, individual reports, Team Intelligence and an AI Companion. Access depends on the relevant offer and account permissions. Review individual offers and team offers for details. Existing functionality is not evidence that enterprise integrations have shipped.

In development

NBIF methodology refinement and validation preparation are documented development work. The AI readiness approach on this site is a proposed assessment structure. A separate enterprise human context layer has no verified release milestone published here.

Planned

The long-term concept connects enterprise systems, AI assistants, organizational context, human readiness, team structure and leadership context. Any implementation would need scoped permissions, auditable access, data minimization and a way to correct or withdraw context.

An illustrative future workflow

An assistant preparing a team meeting could receive authorized, relevant team-level context and the purpose of the meeting. It should not receive raw individual diagnostic responses by default. That scenario illustrates the intended design direction; it is not a demonstration of a released integration.

Integration status

No implemented Model Context Protocol (MCP) server or connector is established by this repository audit. No MCP integration, enterprise SSO, permission synchronization or production service-level commitment is promised here.

Discuss a bounded pilot and its data requirements through Contact. Read Methodology before using diagnostic context in decisions.
