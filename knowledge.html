AI readiness and adoption knowledge

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

Practical explanations of the human and organizational conditions for AI use. Each guide distinguishes recommendations from research findings and links to the relevant assessment or inquiry route.

  • What is AI readiness?

    A concise definition of AI readiness, its organizational and human dimensions, and how it differs from AI adoption.

  • How do you measure AI readiness?

    Measure company AI readiness with task evidence, interviews and an action register rather than a survey score alone.

  • Why do AI transformations fail?

    Investigate AI implementation risks through workflow fit, responsibility, evaluation and employee support.

  • What prevents employees from adopting AI?

    Identify AI adoption barriers and prepare employees through practical support, participation and workflow evaluation.

  • How does leadership affect AI adoption?

    Clarify leadership responsibilities for AI priorities, review rules, learning time and employee feedback.
