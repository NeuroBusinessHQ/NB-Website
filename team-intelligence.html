Team Intelligence and the NeuroBusiness Team Map

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

NeuroBusiness Team Intelligence uses business workstyle assessments to support conversations about team roles, communication and collective perspectives. It is a reflection and development approach, not a ranking of people’s value or ability.

Existing Team Scan offer

The current team offer describes confidential individual participation, an aggregated team profile, a Team Intelligence Report, a contribution matrix and communication or task-briefing guides. See the Team Scan offer and inquiry flow for scope and terms.

What “Team Map” means here

Team Map is a descriptive name for making the team’s working patterns and contributions visible. This page does not claim that a separate product under that name has been released. The implemented offer is Team Intelligence. Use the map to formulate questions, then check them against actual work.

Example: a delayed decision

Record the decision, required evidence, decision owner and people affected. Ask whether the delay reflects missing information, unclear authority or incompatible expectations. Use the profile as a conversation aid, then agree a concrete decision rule and review it after use.

Boundaries and AI adoption

Do not infer AI competence from a workstyle type. Combine team reflection with task-based evidence, access checks and an AI readiness assessment plan. Aggregate findings can still be sensitive in small teams; agree who can see what before participation.
