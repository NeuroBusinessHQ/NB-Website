Human-centered AI adoption and consulting

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

AI adoption is the process of integrating AI into everyday work with the skills, responsibilities and checks needed to use it well. Human-centered adoption involves the people doing the work in choosing, testing and improving the system.

What AI adoption consulting should address

A useful engagement links a business problem to workflow design, practical training and a decision about scale. It should identify who will review AI outputs, who can approve access, how employees can report problems and what evidence would justify changing course.

A working sequence for a company pilot

  1. Choose a task with a clear owner and reviewable output.
  2. Map the current workflow and baseline quality, effort and error rates.
  3. Ask affected employees what makes the task difficult and what support is missing.
  4. Introduce an approved tool with example tasks and explicit review rules.
  5. Compare performance and experience with the baseline, then revise or stop.

Support people without labeling them

Low use can reflect poor task fit, lack of time, tool friction or well-founded concern. Treat these as questions to investigate. A workstyle profile must not become a label for resistance or a proxy for employability.
