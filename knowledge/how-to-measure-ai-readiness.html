How do you measure AI readiness?

By NeuroBusiness · Editorial review: 12 October 2026 · Evidence policy

Measure AI readiness by combining evidence from real tasks, employee and leadership interviews, workflow documentation and technical checks. Assess the conditions for a specific use case, record uncertainty and link each gap to a responsible owner.

How can companies assess AI readiness?

  1. Define the decision: identify the workflow and the proposed AI assistance. Specify what remains a human responsibility.
  2. Establish a baseline: record current quality, effort, common errors and the review process.
  3. Collect complementary evidence: ask people what they need, observe a representative task and check whether approved tools and information are accessible.
  4. Review conditions: distinguish missing evidence from an observed gap. Agree what must happen before piloting.
  5. Reassess: repeat the same task and checks after intervention. Document changed conditions and new limitations.

How do you measure organizational AI readiness?

Examine the system around employees. Does an accountable owner exist? Is there a safe route for data use? Can errors be detected and escalated? Is support available? Review evidence from the workflow as well as responses from different roles. Do not average away a critical access or governance gap.

Interpretation and limitations

Self-report measures confidence and experience, not necessarily task performance. A small pilot cannot establish population norms. Report what was observed, who participated and which roles or conditions were not tested. Compare findings over time only when scope and measurement remain comparable.

Reference and next step

NIST AI Risk Management Framework (2023) is relevant to risk identification and review. The evidence register is a proposed NeuroBusiness working method; it is not a NIST assessment. See framework status and assessment scope.
